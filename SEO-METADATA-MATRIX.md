## 1. Overview & Strategy

This document serves as the single source of truth for the approved metadata across all 19 core indexable pages on Tokriii.com (10 core & CMS routes + 9 product categories). 

### Optimization Principles Applied
1. **Title Length:** 50–60 characters; front-loaded with primary commercial intent followed by branding (`· Tokriii`).
2. **Meta Description:** 140–160 characters; actionable value proposition highlighting fresh handpicked fruit, Azadpur Mandi sourcing, morning delivery before 7 AM, and Delhi NCR / Gurugram coverage.
3. **Semantic Hierarchy:** Exactly one logical `<h1>` per page.
4. **Canonical Strategy:** Self-referencing HTTPS canonicals strictly on the authoritative production host (`https://tokriii.com`).
5. **Open Graph & Twitter/X:** Full absolute URLs for images (`https://tokriii.com/...`), `summary_large_image` Twitter cards.
6. **Structured Data:** Schema.org JSON-LD tailored to page type (`Organization`, `OnlineStore`, `Store`, `WebSite`, `CollectionPage`, `ItemList`, `Product`, `Offer`, `BreadcrumbList`, `BlogPosting`, `FAQPage`).
7. **Strict Grounding:** Zero invented ratings, fake discounts, or synthetic delivery areas.
